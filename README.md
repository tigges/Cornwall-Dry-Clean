# Cornwall Dry Cleaners

A simple one-page website for the family-run dry cleaners at 23 Haven Lane, Ealing.

## What the Google listing told us

The [Google Business listing](https://maps.app.goo.gl/cw7sznF2TJq2SkL69) is the shop’s real homepage today. It is a 4.9-star dry cleaner in Ealing, not a shop in Cornwall.

- **Name:** Cornwall Dry Cleaners
- **Address:** 23 Haven Lane, Ealing, London W5 2HZ
- **Phone:** 020 8991 9413
- **Rating:** 4.9 / 5 from 45 Google reviews
- **Hours:** Monday–Friday 8am–6pm, Saturday 9am–5pm, Sunday closed
- **Company:** Cornwall Dry Cleaners Limited, on Haven Lane since 2003

Reviews keep repeating the same strengths: careful dry cleaning, stubborn stain removal, repairs and alterations, next-day turnaround, laundry for Airbnb hosts, and a family counter people mention by name (Gina). Several reviewers call the shop a hidden gem on a residential street near Ealing Broadway.

## What the site is for

The listing already has trust. It does not have a clear place to send people. This page does three jobs:

1. Make the shop easy to find on Haven Lane.
2. Show services in plain language, taken from what customers already praise.
3. Make calling and getting directions the main actions.

No booking system, no fake email, no extra pages. A neighbourhood shop converts on the phone and at the counter.
