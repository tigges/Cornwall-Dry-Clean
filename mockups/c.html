A hidden shop, not a chain

Tucked away on a residential street — and easy to miss if you only look on the Broadway.

Cornwall Dry Cleaners has been on Haven Lane since 2003. It is a family-run counter, not a high-street franchise: the same people take your clothes in, check them before you collect, and remember the regulars.

Google reviews keep coming back to the same things. Careful work on wool and formal wear. Stains that looked permanent. Repairs done without being asked. And Gina, who customers describe as the reason they keep walking down the lane.

If you are near Ealing Broadway station, Haven Green or the side streets of W5, we are closer than you think.
